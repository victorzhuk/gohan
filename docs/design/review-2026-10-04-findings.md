# Review findings, 2026-10-04

The cross-spec findings of the 2026-10-04 review, one row per contradiction, exactly as the lenses returned them. Rows marked `closed (ADR-0138)` were the first reconciliation; rows marked `pass 5 (ADR-0140)` were reconciled by the second, except where pass 5 recorded a different resolution (a field on a result instead of a new enum member, or a name for a window another rule already bounded). A verification lens re-read every edited site independently; its residuals are listed under *Spec consistency debt* in `docs/design/risks.md`. `file:line` refers to the corpus as the lens read it, so a line number may have moved since.
